Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Overview

This policy explains what information Ekinox (the "Bot") collects when you use it on Discord, why, and how you can control it. We collect only what the Bot needs to work.

2. Information we collect

  • Discord IDs: user, server, channel and role IDs needed to run commands and remember settings.
  • Server settings: configuration chosen by server administrators, such as enabled features or channels.
  • Feature data: data created by using Bot features, such as stats, levels or preferences linked to your user ID.
  • Command usage: which commands are used and when, plus error logs, to fix bugs and prevent abuse.

Message content is only processed when a command or feature you use needs it, and is not kept longer than that feature requires.

3. How we use it

  • to provide, run and improve the Bot's features;
  • to save your and your server's settings;
  • to fix errors, keep the Bot secure and prevent spam or abuse.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your data. It is not shared with third parties, except the services needed to host the Bot or when required by law. All data passes through Discord, which has its own Privacy Policy.

5. Storage and retention

Data is stored on secured servers and kept only as long as the features you use need it. When the Bot is removed from a server, that server's data may be deleted.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to show or delete the data linked to your Discord account at any time. Contact us through our support server and we will handle the request within 30 days.

7. Children

The Bot is not meant for anyone under the minimum age allowed by Discord's Terms of Service. We do not knowingly collect data from such users.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
